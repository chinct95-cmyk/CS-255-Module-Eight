The DriverPass project is about bridging the gap between teens of the new age and being able to sign up for driving lessons efficiently. The client, DriverPass, would like to get a program or application created where students can go online and easily and securely sign up for driving lessons with a real driving instructor to increase their chances of passing their driver's license test the first time around. The app will also have online exams, real time feedback from their instructors, and other tools that can help students become model drivers! 
I think I did a good job at understanding and empathizing with what the client wants for their product. I personally failed my drivers test twice before I was able to get my license. So I understand the pain of not having the tools needed that could have saved time, stress, and money available. I also did a good job at taking technical concepts and breaking them down and translating them so individuals who are not tech savvy are able to understand every step of the way.
A few things I would like to revise would be a couple of the UML diagrams. I wasn't too sure when creating some of them and meeting all the requirements for submission. I since have learned from my mistakes especially after seeing examples from my fellow classmates on what my diagrams should look like in the future.
I broke down all of the user's needs and then converted them to a visual medium with the UML diagrams. When designing, it is of the utmost importance to meet the users needs. The only reason I am designing is because of the user. They put in a request for a service and most likely paid good money to get these steps complete on their behalf. It is important to be sure the users needs are met in order to get the job done as well as making the user feel heard and represented.
I approach design software pretty cautiously. Depending on the client I would have to translate the terminology and steps to a more simple and casual format. On the other end I could have to make the design more technical and fit the provisions set by the client. It is a case by case basis, but since my mind works technically, I approach designs with caution. Not everyone's mind works the same so I keep that in mind.
